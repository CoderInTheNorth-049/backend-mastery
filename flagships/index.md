# Flagships

Two long-running projects that turn the curriculum into a portfolio centerpiece. Each flagship has a **project brief** and a sequence of **milestones** that map to the phases you have completed. Reference implementations live under `solution/` in the repo and are intentionally hidden from this website.

| Flagship                                                                 | Est. time   | Why it matters                                                                                                                      |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [1 — Webhook delivery platform](webhook-delivery-platform/project-brief) | 60–90 hours | A distributed-systems product: retries, backoff, dead-letter queues, HMAC signing, idempotency, multi-tenancy, and a live dashboard |
| [2 — Event-driven order system](order-saga-system/project-brief)         | 50–80 hours | Kafka sagas, transactional outbox, compensations, and CQRS read models — the senior system-design interview territory               |

## How to work through a flagship

1. Read the **project brief** to understand the product and architecture.
2. Open the **milestones** index and work through them in order.
3. Finish each milestone before moving on; the build guides call out the phase skills you need.
4. Keep a `notes.md` with decisions, trade-offs, and incidents — it becomes interview material.

Both flagships are designed to be extended as you progress through phases 06–10.
