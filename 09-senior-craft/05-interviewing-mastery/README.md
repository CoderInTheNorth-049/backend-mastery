# Topic: Interviewing mastery — your stories, your rubric

> **Goal:** Convert this repo's war rooms into interview ammunition. You'll self-assess one of your phase-05 designs against a real rubric and build a six-story behavioral bank where every story is TRUE because you lived it here.

- 🎥 YouTube search: **“system design interview walkthrough”** — watch one AFTER recording yourself (compare honestly)

## Core concepts

| Concept | Must be able to answer | Free resources |
|---|---|---|
| The SD rubric | problem scoping → requirements → estimation → high-level design → deep dives → trade-off defense. Practice against EACH axis | [phase-05 template](../../05-system-design/07-classic-designs/template.md) is the rubric in disguise |
| STAR format | Situation, Task, Action, Result — with a MEASURED result | common behavioral framing |
| Story bank | 6 reusable stories cover ~90% of behavioral questions; extract from retros, not imagination | your `notes.md` files |
| "Weakness" questions | pick a real growth edge + the system you built to manage it (not "I work too hard") | universal guidance |
| Asking clarifying questions | senior signal #1 in SD interviews: interrogate requirements before drawing | phase-05 prompts trained this |

## Keep in mind

- **Interviewers remember specifics** — "I hit a circular-dependency wall building a DI container" beats "I'm persistent."
- **Recorded mock loops feel cringe and work.** One recording reveals filler words, skipped estimations, and missed clarifying questions better than ten reads.
- **Every story needs a failure mode you'd now avoid** — redemption arcs are memorable and honest.
- **Numbers are confidence**: budgets burned, p95s improved, coverage %s — you have them from phases 04–08.

## Self-check quiz

<details><summary>1. Interviewer asks “tell me about a hard technical decision.” Which repo moment fits best?</summary>
Flagship saga pivot choice or outbox-vs-CDC — any decision with ≥2 defensible options, quantified consequences, and a rollback plan. Structure it as options→decision→outcome.</details>

<details><summary>2. Why rehearse stories instead of improvising?</summary>
Improvised answers ramble and skip measured results. Rehearsed skeletons (30s each) free working memory for follow-ups — which is where seniors differentiate.</details>

<details><summary>3. Your mock recording shows zero clarifying questions before drawing boxes. Fix?</summary>
Force a script: three questions minimum (scale, consistency need, read/write pattern) written on the template's line 1 before anything else.</details>
